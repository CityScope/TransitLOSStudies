# City science flex fits: results

Generated by `flex/fit_cities.py report` from `flex/results/v2_*.json` (revision of 2026-11-25). Model and rules: `flex/fit_cities.py` and `tools/studytools/flexfit.py` (`SmoothModel`).

The score is computed per H3 res-11 cell, `s = log(1 + A / eps)`, and a unit takes the population-weighted mean of its cells' scores. Smooth log-logistic curves (bounded steepness) for headway, speed and walking; rail >= tram >= bus by construction; the speed curve is fitted first with every mode factor 1, then frozen, then the mode factors. Every number is held out (within-city 5-fold, spatial). **R²** = share of the variance of the units' transit share explained (people-weighted); **controls only** = the same without any service term.
