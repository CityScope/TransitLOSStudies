# ASSUMED feed: intercity and suburban rail of the Shanghai metro area

Not official data. China Railway services that the CPTOND dataset does not contain (it holds only Ningbo S1, kept in the main feed).

* **Huning**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track between stations, 120 km/h average incl. stops; 5 min at peak (minimum headway 5 min) and 8 min otherwise, 05:45-23:00 (110-142 pairs a day on the intercity line, Pengpai / Changzhou government / Jiangsu news; Suzhou station alone has 192 train pairs a day in the Jan 2026 plan, Wikipedia)
* **Jinghu HSR**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 160 km/h average incl. stops; every 11 min 06:00-22:00 from about 98.5 pairs a day on the Shanghai-Nanjing section (one self-media article; LOW CONFIDENCE, trains skip some of these stations)
* **Hu-Ning conventional**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 100 km/h average incl. stops; every 18 min 06:00-22:00 from the 57 pairs of conventional passenger trains a day on the Hu-Ning section of the Jinghu railway (Wikipedia Suzhou station, Jan 2026 plan; 10 EMU pairs besides); most of them stop only at the big stations (MEDIUM-LOW CONFIDENCE); Shanghai South's conventional trains moved to Songjiang station on 2025-07-01 and are not modelled
* **Hu-Kun conventional**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 90 km/h average incl. stops; every 20 min 06:00-22:00: Songjiang station handles 77 conventional trains a day (official, Q1 2025; it took over Shanghai South's conventional trains on 2025-07-01) and Jiaxing station 154 trains a day (railway timetable site); ESTIMATE, most long-distance trains stop only at the big stations (LOW CONFIDENCE)
* **Songjiang-Shanghai South shuttle**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap; C500 leaves Songjiang 06:48 (20 min) and C3046 07:59 (16 min) towards Shanghai South (Shanghai Bendibao timetable notice); the two morning trains are modelled as one every 70 min 06:45-08:15; the return trains are NOT published in what I found: one every 60 min 17:30-19:30 is an ESTIMATE (LOW CONFIDENCE); 75 km/h (about 25 km in 20 min)
* **Xuan-Hang / Ning-Hang conventional**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap; every 120 min 06:00-21:00 ESTIMATE from about 7 pairs a day: Guangde station, on the same line in Anhui, has 14 stopping trains a day (railway timetable site), an old travel page says 6 trains a day pass Huzhou, and the stopping counts of 87 at Anji, 81 at Deqing and 58 at Changxing South are mostly high-speed trains of other lines; the conventional Nanjing-Hangzhou trains run over this line (no separate Ning-Hang conventional line exists in the area); LOW CONFIDENCE
* **Ning-Hang HSR**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track; every 15 min 06:30-22:00 ESTIMATE: Huzhou station has 288 stopping trains a day (railway timetable site) shared with other services, Deqing 81 and Changxing South 58 (the line's stations are served by only part of the trains); LOW CONFIDENCE
* **Xiao-Yong conventional**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap; every 60 min 06:00-21:00 ESTIMATE: Yuyao station had 16 conventional pairs a day, all through trains from Ningbo (Wikipedia, June 2017) and 62 trains a day stop there now, of which 9 pairs are the S1 intercity and the Hangzhou-Ningbo C636; LOW CONFIDENCE
* **Hangzhou-Ningbo HSR**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 180 km/h average incl. stops; every 7 min 06:00-22:30: Ningbo station has 416 stopping trains a day (about 208 a direction, railway timetable site) and 103-118 direct trains run Ningbo-Hangzhou East (Qunar / Ctrip snapshots, 2026; fastest 41-54 min) (MEDIUM-LOW CONFIDENCE: Yuyao North is served by only some trains)
* **Yong-Tai-Wen HSR**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 130 km/h average incl. stops (Ninghai-Ningbo about 30 min); every 15 min 06:20-22:00 as the rest of the 416 trains a day at Ningbo station (no count for this line; LOW CONFIDENCE)
* **Nanjing-Qidong railway (Nantong)**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 110 km/h average incl. stops; every 12 min 07:00-22:00: Nantong station has 212 stopping trains a day (about 106 a direction, railway timetable site) and the Nanjing-Qidong line had 41.5 pairs from 2019-10 (Xinhua Daily) (LOW CONFIDENCE, only part of the trains stop at Baipu)
* **Nanjing-Qidong railway (Qidong)**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 110 km/h average; every 90 min 07:00-22:00 from the 6 pairs a day on Qidong-Nantong (Xinhua Daily 2019; LOW CONFIDENCE)
* **Hu-Su-Tong**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 130 km/h average incl. stops; every 25 min 06:00-22:30 (37-43 pairs a day at Taicang, Changshu and Zhangjiagang at opening in 2020, Bendibao / SASAC)
* **Hu-Su-Hu HSR**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 200 km/h average incl. stops (Hongqiao-Huzhou 55 min, Songjiang-Huzhou East 37 min, Hongqiao-Nanxun 43 min in the opening reports); every 15 min 06:00-22:00: 38 trains a day at opening in Dec 2024, 229 a day network-wide from Q1 2025, and 127 trains a day between Nanxun and Hangzhou West on 2026-11-02 (Qunar) (LOW CONFIDENCE: no per-station count for the Shanghai side)
* **Huhang**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap (Songjiang South not located), straight track, 150 km/h average incl. stops; every 7 min 06:00-22:30 (Jiaxing South has 299 stopping trains a day, i.e. about 150 a direction, railway timetable site; timetable sites list 168-178 trains between Hongqiao and Hangzhou East; the earlier 13 min came from the 80 pairs of the 2010 plan; LOW CONFIDENCE: no per-station count found)
* **Jinshan**: ASSUMED: stations from OpenStreetMap, straight track, 65 km/h average incl. stops; every 15 min 05:30-22:00 (10-20 min, 47 trains a day, first 05:30 and last 21:57; Baidu Zhidao / Sohu 2025 timetable)

Not covered: the Xin-Chang railway (Wikipedia: only Xinyi-Haian carries passenger trains, none in this area) and conventional trains of lines without a count (Ning-Hang conventional), the Hangzhou-Huzhou part of Hu-Su-Hu beyond Huzhou East, the trains of Shanghai Station, Shanghai East and Songjiang South (not found in OpenStreetMap), and the Hangzhou-Haining intercity line. Stops are placed at the OSM station nodes; trains are modelled as one frequency-based service per corridor.
